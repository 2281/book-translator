Chapter 1. The Beginning

The morning sun rose slowly over the quiet village. A young traveler stood at the edge of the forest, holding an old map in his hands.

Chapter 2. The Road

The path through the forest was narrow and dark. Tall trees covered the sky, and the air smelled of rain and earth.

Chapter 3. The River

After several hours, he reached a small river. The water was clear and cold. An old wooden bridge crossed the river.
